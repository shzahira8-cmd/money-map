📚 Station 01 · Understand

Learn It! 💗

Let's learn how to add money using Malaysian Ringgit (RM) and sen. Follow the steps and remember to line up the decimal points!

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

1. Identify money values

Recognise and read amounts in RM and sen.

2. Add money

Add two or more money amounts accurately.

3. Use place value

Align ringgit, decimal points and sen correctly.

4. Solve daily problems

Apply money addition in shopping situations.

🧮 How to Add Money

Follow these four simple steps.

Step 1Write the amounts clearly.
Step 2Align the decimal points.
Step 3Add the sen and ringgit.
Step 4Check your final answer.

✨ Worked Example

Aina buys a pencil for RM2.50 and an eraser for RM1.25. How much does she spend altogether?

RM2.50 + RM1.25 = RM3.75

Answer: Aina spends RM3.75 altogether.

Show the calculation

50 sen + 25 sen = 75 sen.

RM2 + RM1 = RM3.

Total = RM3.75.
